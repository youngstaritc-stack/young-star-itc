# YOUNG STAR ITC — MASTER RULE BOOK

> This document contains only rules already defined/approved for YOUNG STAR ITC. No additional trading rules are introduced here.

## Chapter 1 — Trend

### TR-001 — Trend Change Rule
Trend change is governed by the approved market-structure/trend-change logic. No extra confirmation rule is added here.

### TR-002 — Trend Start Confirmation
Trend start must follow the approved trend-start confirmation process.

### TR-003 — 100% Trend Confirmation
A trend is treated as fully confirmed only when the approved trend confirmation conditions are satisfied.

### TR-004 — Sideways Market Filter
Sideways market conditions must be filtered and must not be treated as a normal directional trend.

## Chapter 2 — Fibonacci Retracement

### FIB-001 — Main Swing Selection
Select the valid main swing according to the approved swing-selection rule.

### FIB-002 — New Swing Start
When a new valid swing starts, the Fibonacci reference must be updated according to the approved swing rule.

### FIB-003 — Virgin Fibonacci Count
Virgin retracements must be identified and counted according to the approved Virgin Fib rule. Invalid/used Fibonacci references must not be treated as Virgin.

### FIB-004 — Priority Zones
The approved Fibonacci retracement priority levels are **38%, 50%, and 61%**. These are the important retest zones.

## Chapter 3 — Fibonacci Extension

### FEX-001 — Third Point
The extension must use the approved third-point rule.

### FEX-002 — 1.618
1.618 is an approved Fibonacci Extension target.

### FEX-003 — 2.618
2.618 is an approved Fibonacci Extension target.

### FEX-004 — 3.618
3.618 is an approved Fibonacci Extension target.

### FEX-005 — 4.618 Final Target
4.618 is the approved final Fibonacci Extension target.

Approved extension sequence:
**0% → 1% → 1.618 → 2.618 → 3.618 → 4.618 Final**

## Chapter 4 — Order Block

### OB-001 — High Quality Order Block
Only Order Blocks satisfying the approved High Quality OB criteria are eligible for consideration.

### OB-002 — Invalid Order Block
An Order Block that satisfies the approved invalidation condition must be rejected.

### OB-003 — Multiple Order Blocks
When multiple Order Blocks exist, priority must follow the approved OB priority rule.

## Chapter 5 — Parallel Channel

### PC-001 — Candle Body Zone
The Parallel Channel zone is defined from the candle **open to close** according to the approved PC rule.

### PC-002 — Shadow Break Invalidation
If the candle high/low shadow breaks the approved PC boundary, the corresponding PC setup is invalid.

## Chapter 6 — Candle Rules

### CDL-001 — JON Candle
The approved JON candle PC rule uses the **25% / 50% / 75% shadow** conditions.

### CDL-002 — Wick
Wick behavior must follow the approved Wick rule.

### CDL-003 — Doji
A Doji is treated as a **normal candle** under the approved rule.

## Chapter 7 — Liquidity & FVG

### LQ-001 — Liquidity
Liquidity is marked for informational/analytical use.

### LQ-002 — Volume Priority
When comparing Liquidity areas, the higher-volume Liquidity receives priority according to the approved rule.

### FVG-001 — FVG Ahead
FVG Ahead may be identified and reported as informational context.

### BOUNDARY-001 — No Entry Signal
**LQ, FVG, and Volume are NOT entry signals and NOT confirmation tools.** They are retained as bot knowledge/information to warn the user before entry.

## Trend Pullback Rule

The last pullback of a trend has an **Order Block candle at the start of the pullback**, and that pullback retests the approved **38% / 50% / 61% Fibonacci zone**.

## Chapter 9 — Take Profit

### TP-001 — Trending Market
Use the approved Trending Market TP rule.

### TP-002 — Side/Base Market
Use the approved Side/Base Market TP rule.

## Implementation Boundary

The AI engine must implement only the rules defined/approved in this Rule Book and previously approved YOUNG STAR ITC rules. No new trading rule, indicator-based confirmation, entry condition, or strategy assumption may be added without explicit approval.
